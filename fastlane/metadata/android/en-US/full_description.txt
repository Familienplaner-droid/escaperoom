You wake up in a strange study. The door is locked. You don't know who you are. In your hand: a crumpled note.

Questory is an escape-room puzzle adventure. Every story is a book, and every chapter is a room you have to escape from.

VILLA MORGENROT – A STORY IN FIVE CHAPTERS
A house by the sea. A grandfather who is waiting. And a memory you win back room by room – from the study through the library and the cellar all the way up into the lighthouse.

WHAT AWAITS YOU
• Lovingly painted rooms full of detail
• Find, examine and combine items
• Codes, locks, secret compartments, a game of chess and Morse code
• Fair puzzles: every solution is hidden in the room – no guessing needed
• Tiered hints: first a gentle nudge, the solution only at the very end
• A moving story about family and memory
• Progress is saved automatically
• Playable in English, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese

HINTS
One hint per chapter is free. More hints are available by watching a short ad.
